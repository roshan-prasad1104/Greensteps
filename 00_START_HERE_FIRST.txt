================================================================================
                     POINTS CALCULATION
================================================================================

Formula:
   points = (distance × 0.1) × multiplier + streak_bonus

Multipliers:
   • Eco-friendly (Walk, Bicycle, EV, Bus): 1.5x
   • Other (Car, Bike): 1.0x

Streak Bonuses:
   • 5+ consecutive days: +1 point
   • 7+ consecutive days: +2 points
   • 15+ consecutive days: +3 points
   • 30+ consecutive days: +5 points

Examples:
   • Walk 5 km: (5 × 0.1) × 1.5 = 0.75 points
   • Drive 10 km: (10 × 0.1) × 1.0 = 1.0 point
   • Bus 8 km: (8 × 0.1) × 1.5 = 1.2 points
   • + 7-day streak: +2 bonus points
